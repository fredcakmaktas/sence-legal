Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Previous versions: https://legal.sencetravel.com

§1 Short summary

  • The AI Feature generates text such as trip suggestions, questions and answers about places and Hotels, and place descriptions. Google's Gemini model generates the text; some summaries about Hotels come from Nuitée. In this version the AI Feature is turned off (§5); the rules in this summary and below apply when it is turned on.
  • We show you before the first interaction that you are interacting with AI. Every AI-generated text, including the summary on place detail pages, is labelled "Generated with AI".
  • It is optional. We ask before your data first goes to Google or Nuitée; you can turn it off in Settings.
  • What you write and earlier messages in the chat go to Google; your questions about Hotels go to Nuitée. We do not add your name, e-mail address, Account ID or IP address; anything you type yourself does go.
  • Outputs may be wrong and are not offers. Price and terms are confirmed only in the Booking steps and the Pre-Contract Information.
  • The AI Feature does not decide about you or make Bookings for you.
  • You can send us a wrong or harmful output using the "Report" option.
  • This Notice is an annex to the Terms of Use. Its sections on personal data (§4, §5, §9 and §10) are provided for your information only; accepting the Terms does not count as consent to those sections.

§2 What the AI Feature is

(1) The AI Feature consists of our features that generate text with artificial intelligence, such as trip suggestions, questions and answers about places and Hotels, and place descriptions. In this version the AI Feature is switched off: the areas where you can ask questions are visible, but every question receives the same fixed text; no AI text is generated and no data is sent to Google (Gemini) or to Nuitée's AI service.

(2) Outputs are not offers. Price, availability and cancellation terms are confirmed only in the Booking steps and the Pre-Contract Information.

(3) The AI Feature does not make Bookings or start payments on your behalf, and it does not make decisions about you (§8).

(4) Using the AI Feature is optional; the rest of the App works if you do not use it.

(5) The accuracy and limits of outputs are covered in §6.

§3 Interacting with AI and labelling of generated content

(1) Once the feature is switched on, when you use the AI Feature, we clearly show you before the first interaction that you are interacting with an AI system, not a human.

(2) Once the feature is switched on, every AI-generated text is labelled "Generated with AI" where it is shown. This includes the AI summary on place detail pages and Hotel summaries.

(3) Once the feature is switched on, this text will also carry a machine-readable marker, so that it can also be technically identified as AI-generated. In this version the AI Feature is switched off and no AI text is generated, so there is no AI text to be marked.

(4) If you share an AI output in the App, you are responsible for what you share and the Community Guidelines apply. We recommend that you state that it was generated with AI.

(5) You can send us an AI output you find offensive, wrong or harmful without leaving the App, using the "Report" option next to the response (Community Guidelines §8). In this version the AI Feature is switched off: every question receives the same fixed text, and because no AI response is generated this option is not offered. You can report a problem to destek@sencetravel.com.

(6) We use safety settings to prevent AI responses from generating content of the kinds in the Community Guidelines §4. We also use reported outputs to improve these settings. In this version, because the AI Feature is switched off, no request is sent to Google (Gemini) or to Nuitée's AI service.

Additional rules by region

  • EU/EEA: This disclosure and marking fall under Article 50 of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), which has applied since 2 August 2026.
  • Türkiye: If we use AI in our advertising, we state this clearly.

§4 What data goes where

(1) Data sent to Google: the text you write to the AI Feature and earlier messages in the same chat; when you ask about a place or a description is generated on a place detail page, its name, category and city; when you ask for suggestions, dates, budget, number of people, departure and destination, interests, travel style (e.g. family, solo) and, if you separately choose it, a summary of your past Bookings; and the language in which the answer is to be generated. In this version none of this data is sent to Google: the AI Feature is switched off and no suggestion request is made.

(2) Data sent to Nuitée: if you ask about a Hotel, your question and the Hotel's identifier.

(3) Not sent: we do not send your name, e-mail address, Account ID or IP address to the AI provider; anything you type yourself does go (see (4)). Your Account ID or IP address is used only within Sencé to apply the daily usage limit.

(4) Do not include your own or others' special categories of data, identity or card details in your questions; everything you type is passed to the provider.

(5) We ask for your permission before your data is first sent to an AI provider (§5). The providers' roles, the country of processing and retention periods are in the Privacy Policy §9, §10 and §11.

§5 Permission, turning off and withdrawal

(1) In this version the AI Feature is switched off for all Users and cannot be turned on; no AI description is generated on the place detail, a fixed text is shown in that area and no data goes to Google. If the feature is switched on, before you first use it we show you this screen (Explicit Consent Texts R3):

If you turn it on, what you write, earlier messages in the conversation and the answer language; if you ask for suggestions, the budget, dates, number of people, departure point and destination and interests you enter, and your travel style; and, if you ask about a place, that place's name, category and city are sent to Google (Gemini, USA) to generate a response. A question you ask about a Hotel is sent to Nuitée to be answered. We do not add your name, email or IP address; anything you type yourself is sent. Details are in the AI Notice.

§6 Accuracy, limits and reliance on outputs

(1) AI outputs are suggestions. They may contain wrong, incomplete or out-of-date information and may invent a place, price or rule that does not exist.

(2) Do not rely on an output alone for visas, passports, health, safety or legal rules; check official sources.

(3) Check an AI output before you make a decision. For important matters, rely on official sources and on information from the Hotel and Nuitée.

(4) If an AI output differs from the information shown in the Booking steps, the Pre-Contract Information or the Booking confirmation, the information in those documents applies to the Booking. A price range shown in AI suggestions is only an estimate, is marked "estimated" on screen and is not a Hotel's selling price; the selling price is shown only in the Booking steps. In this version no AI suggestions are generated and the fixed sample suggestion list is not shown either.

(5) We are responsible for providing the AI Feature with care and for fixing known errors. If you notice a wrong or misleading output, tell us (§3 (5) or destek@sencetravel.com); we will review it within a reasonable time and correct or remove it where necessary.

(6) We are liable for loss you suffer because of an AI output in accordance with the law and in proportion to our fault. Our liability for intent and gross negligence, and for death and personal injury, is not limited. Your statutory rights as a consumer remain unaffected (Terms of Use §17).

(7) You are responsible for using an AI output in a way that harms others (Terms of Use §12).

§7 Prohibited use

(1) The prohibited content and conduct in the Community Guidelines §4 also apply to the AI Feature.

(2) Do not use the AI Feature to generate content of these kinds or to bypass safety measures. For example, do not use it to generate unlawful content, content sexualising children, non-consensual intimate content or fakes, content inciting hatred or violence, harassment, fraud or impersonation, other people's personal data, or content infringing intellectual property rights.

(3) We recommend that you do not share your special categories of data (such as health, religion or political opinions); never share anyone else's (§4 (4)).

(4) The Community Guidelines §11–§14 apply to use that breaches these rules.

§8 No automated decisions

(1) We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you or similarly significantly affect you.

(2) AI Feature: Suggestions are generated from the information you give at that moment and, when you ask, a summary of your past Bookings. Suggestions are not decisions; nothing is done without your approval.

(3) Content moderation: In this version we do not use an automated pre-publication filter; a reported post is hidden until an administrator reviews it. Review and how to appeal are explained in the Community Guidelines §10 and §12.

(4) During a Booking, Nuitée carries out its own fraud check. This check is Nuitée's decision and may affect whether the Booking is completed. If you think it was wrongly not completed, write to destek@sencetravel.com; we will pass your request to Nuitée and tell you the outcome. This check and Hotel ranking are explained in the Privacy Policy §18.

(5) If you believe an analysis carried out solely by automated systems has produced an outcome against you, you can object (§10).

§10 Rights and contact

(1) You can also exercise your personal data rights for data processed through the AI Feature: requesting information and access, receiving a copy, rectification, erasure, objecting to processing and objecting to an outcome against you that results from analysis carried out solely by automated systems. The full list of rights for your region is in the Privacy Policy §13.

(2) How to make a request. In the App, from the "Request data copy" row under Settings → Privacy → Your data (it opens a draft e-mail to the support address), or by writing to destek@sencetravel.com from the email address registered with Sencé. If you write from another address, we may ask for additional information to verify your identity; we will not ask for more than is needed. Users in Türkiye may also use the written and KEP channels in the Privacy Policy §13.

(3) Time limits and fees.

  • Under the KVKK we respond within 30 days at the latest.
  • Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR we respond within one month. If the request is complex or there are many requests, this may be extended by two further months; we tell you about the extension and the reason within the first month.
  • If a US state privacy law applies, we respond within 45 days; if necessary this may be extended once by a further 45 days.
  • Requests are free of charge.

(4) Complaints.

  • Türkiye: If your request is refused, you find our response insufficient or we do not respond in time, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of our response and in any case within 60 days of your request. You must have applied to us before complaining to the Board.
  • EU/EEA: You may complain to the data protection supervisory authority in the EU/EEA country where you live, work or where you believe the infringement took place.
  • United Kingdom: You may first complain to us: we acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and keep you informed of the progress and the outcome of our review. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

(5) AI outputs. To report an output, use the "Report" option next to the response (§3 (5)); to tell us about a wrong output, use the route in §6 (5).

(6) Contact. You can reach us about anything at destek@sencetravel.com. This channel is not answered by automated means only. We reply to requests other than personal data requests within 7 days.

(7) We change this notice following the procedure in the Terms of Use §21.
