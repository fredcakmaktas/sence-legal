  • Protecting our rights in legal disputes: Art. 5/2-e.
  • Analytics and Marketing Communications: only with Explicit Consent that you give separately (Art. 5/1).
  • If you write special category information such as health details in the special request field, passing it to the Hotel: Explicit Consent that you give separately (Art. 6/3). Special category information you choose to include in content you share publicly: your making it public (Art. 6/3).
  • Method of collection (§4): through the App and the website, electronically and by automated means. You enter some of the data; Apple, Google and Nuitée send us some of it.
  • Transfers (§5): to Nuitée and the Hotel, to set up the Booking and provide the stay; to our hosting, AI, map, sign-in, Notification, connectivity check, error monitoring, analytics and email providers, to run the Services; to competent authorities, to comply with our legal obligations. We do not ask for your consent for these transfers.
  • Retention (§6): as long as needed for the purpose or required by law. If you delete your Account, your data is erased no later than 30 days after your request (deletion is completed by a daily job that runs after the 30-day waiting period ends, so it may run into day 31). For Booking records we must keep by law, the link to your Account is removed and contact details are erased; the remaining record is pseudonymised, not anonymous. We keep consent records as proof (§6, §8).
  • Your rights (§7, §9): you can exercise your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK by writing to destek@sencetravel.com or through the other channels in §7. We respond within 30 days at the latest. If you find our response insufficient, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
  • Explicit Consent (§8): where Explicit Consent is needed, we ask for it in the Explicit Consent Texts, under a separate heading with a separate statement. Not giving it does not stop you using the Services.

§5.3 Transfers abroad (KVKK Article 9)

Sencé is operated from Türkiye. Your data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt) and some of our service providers are in the US (see §5.2). Your data is therefore transferred abroad.

  • At the date of this notice, the Personal Data Protection Board has not announced an adequacy decision for the countries we transfer data to; we therefore do not rely on an adequacy decision.
  • Each transfer relies on the legal ground we set out for that purpose in the table in §3.
  • As the safeguard, we will use the standard contracts announced by the Board: a "controller to processor" standard contract with providers that process data on our behalf, and a "controller to controller" standard contract with recipients that process your data for their own purposes.
  • As of the publication date of this version, these contracts have not yet been signed or notified to the Personal Data Protection Authority.
  • Onward transfers by our recipients to other countries are subject to the same safeguards. Hotels receive your data from Nuitée, not from us; the transfer to the Hotel is subject to the onward transfer rules in our standard contract with Nuitée. We do not sign a separate contract with each Hotel.
  • These transfers are part of the ordinary operation of the Services and happen regularly. They are therefore not incidental (occasional) transfers and do not rely on your Explicit Consent. We do not ask for your consent for them.
  • If an incidental transfer is needed, we make it only for that transfer and only on one of the grounds in Article 9/6 of the KVKK. If that ground is your Explicit Consent, we explain the possible risks of the transfer in advance and ask for your approval with a separate statement. Example: forwarding a request of yours, at your request, to a body abroad with which we have no regular relationship.
  • You enter your card details directly into Nuitée's payment form; they are not transferred through us.

Copy of the safeguards. You can ask for a copy of the safeguards we use by writing to destek@sencetravel.com. We may remove parts that contain trade secrets.

The transfer safeguards we use for users in the EU/EEA and the United Kingdom are in the "Transfers abroad" section of the Privacy Policy.

§6 Retention

We keep your data for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it is processed, or for the period required by law. When the period ends, we erase or destroy the data or make it anonymous.

  • Pseudonymised data is not anonymous. Data that does not carry your name but can be linked to you with additional information remains personal data. Booking records kept after your Account is deleted, with the link to your Account removed and contact details erased, are of this kind; we protect them as personal data and destroy them when their period ends.
  • Records kept for a statutory period are kept only for that statutory purpose and with restricted access.
  • Periodic destruction. We destroy data whose retention period has ended at regular intervals; the interval is no later than 3 months from when the obligation to destroy arises. We record every destruction and keep these records for at least 3 years.

We have set the retention period and its basis separately for these data groups: Account details; User Content; Moments; direct messages; current location; history of regions you have visited; push token; AI Feature questions and answers; Booking and transaction records; pre-contract information and confirmation records; Request and Complaint System records; invoices, accounting and tax documents; support correspondence; consent records; Marketing Communication opt-out and message records; traffic data; usage limit record; website visitor IP address; error, crash and performance logs; analytics data; Content Report, Moderation Decision and Appeal records; Blocking; destruction records; backups; records subject to a legal dispute; the Booking record held by Nuitée. The detailed table is in the Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy.

How to make a request

  • In the App, from the "Request data copy" row under Settings → Privacy → Your data; it opens a draft e-mail to the support address.
  • By writing to destek@sencetravel.com from the email address registered with Sencé. If you write from another address, we may ask for additional information to verify your identity; we will not ask for more than is needed.
  • In writing to (to be added); by registered electronic mail (KEP) to (to be added); or with a secure electronic signature or mobile signature.
  • Requests under the KVKK are made in Turkish. We also accept requests in English from users outside Türkiye.
  • When you apply through the App or from your registered email address, your name and your request are enough. Written applications under the KVKK procedure also require identity and address details.
  • Your legal representative (parent, guardian) or attorney may apply on your behalf with a document showing their authority.

Time limits and fees

  • Under the KVKK we respond within 30 days at the latest.
  • Requests are free of charge.
  • If we cannot fulfil part of your request for a legal reason, we tell you in writing or electronically which part and why.
  • The additional rights and response times for users in the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and the United States are in the "Your rights and how to make a request" section of the Privacy Policy.

Your rights (KVKK Article 11)

You may ask:

  • to learn whether your data is processed and, if so, to request information about it;
  • to learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose;
  • to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred;
  • to have your data corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
  • to have your data erased or destroyed under the conditions in Article 7 of the KVKK;
  • to have corrections and erasures notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred;
  • to object to an outcome against you that results from analysis carried out solely by automated systems;
  • to claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

You can also send your objection to processing based on legitimate interests in §3 through the same channels.

Account deletion and erasure of specific data. You can delete your Account in the App under Settings → Delete Account. How to have specific data erased without deleting your Account, and which records are kept after deletion, are set out in the Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy.

§8 Processing based on explicit consent

  • We inform you before processing your data. We do not ask you to approve this information; we only record that you have seen it.
  • Sign-up has three separate steps: accepting the Terms of Use, confirming that you have read this privacy notice, and choosing optional permissions.
  • We ask for your Explicit Consent only for analytics, Marketing Communications, and writing special category information such as health details in the special request field.
  • Each consent is presented under a separate heading, with a separate statement and an unticked option. Not consenting does not stop you using the Services; only the related feature does not work.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: by writing to destek@sencetravel.com. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.
  • We record which consent you gave or withdrew, with which text version and when, and keep this as proof even after your Account is deleted (see §6). In this version only your age declaration is recorded among these records; when your Account is deleted, the IP address, browser and device details in the record are cleared, while the event, document, version and time remain.
  • We do not ask for consent for processing based on a contract, the law or legitimate interests (Account, Bookings, transfers to providers abroad, security) (see §3).

The Explicit Consent Texts set out each consent under its own heading: analytics (R1), Marketing Communications (R2) and special category information in the special request field (R6.1). The same document also explains why turning on the AI Feature (R3), location permission (R4) and notification permission (R5) are not Explicit Consent, and how you manage them. Location, camera, photo, microphone and notification permissions are operating system permissions; they are not Explicit Consent under the KVKK.

§9 Complaint to the Board

If your request is refused, you find our response insufficient or we do not respond in time, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of our response and in any case within 60 days of your request (KVKK Article 14). You must have applied to us before complaining to the Board. Website of the Personal Data Protection Authority: https://www.kvkk.gov.tr

§10 Updates

(1) We change this notice only for a valid reason: a change in law, regulation or a court or authority decision; adding, changing or removing a feature; security and fighting abuse; a change in the terms of our providers such as Nuitée; making the text clearer or correcting errors.

(2) We will notify you of a change in the App and by email at least 30 days before it takes effect. The notice will summarise what has changed.

(3) Changes required by law or by an authority's decision, or that address an urgent security risk, may take effect sooner. In that case we will inform you as soon as possible.

(4) This notice is information; we do not ask for your approval of changes. If a change introduces new processing that requires Explicit Consent, we will ask for your Explicit Consent for that processing separately, with a separate statement (see §8).

(5) We state each version's number and effective date at the top of the document. We publish this document and its previous versions at https://legal.sencetravel.com in a form that is public, easily accessible and machine-readable. We record which version you saw and when.
