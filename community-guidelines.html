Sencé Community Guidelines and Content Reporting and Removal Procedure

Version 2.0 · Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Türkçe

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Previous versions: https://legal.sencetravel.com

§1 Short summary

  • You are responsible for what you share. No person reviews content item by item before publication; in this version we do not use an automated pre-publication filter; a reported post is hidden until an administrator reviews it, and we act when we learn that content is unlawful or breaches these rules (§10).
  • Prohibited content and conduct are listed in §4. We have no tolerance for objectionable content or harassment.
  • Anyone can report a post, comment, Moment, message, profile, Review or AI response using the "Report" option or by writing to destek@sencetravel.com.
  • A person reviews reports. We remove an intimate image of you shared without your consent within 48 hours at the latest of your valid report (§10).
  • If we hide or remove your content or restrict your Account, in this version we do not send you a Notification. You can Appeal free of charge by writing to destek@sencetravel.com for at least six months after you learn of it. You can still go to the courts and competent authorities.
  • You can block someone who bothers you.
  • Only the Booking Holder of a Booking made through the App can review that Hotel, after the stay dates.

(2) Requests and complaints about Bookings, payment, cancellation and stays are not covered by this document but by the Request and Complaint System (Terms of Use §26). Requests about your personal data are explained in the Privacy Policy §13.

(3) The order of precedence between documents is in the Terms of Use §4.

§3 Core principles

(1) You create User Content such as posts, comments, Moments, messages, profile details and Reviews. You are responsible for the content you share and for its compliance with the law and the Community Guidelines.

(2) When you share content, you confirm that: you have the right to share it; you have the permission of the people who can be recognised in it; it is accurate to the best of your knowledge; and you will disclose any benefit you received for it (§6).

(3) Before you first post content, write a comment or send a message, you confirm in a separate step that you accept the Community Guidelines.

(4) Content is not reviewed item by item by a person before it is published; in this version we do not use an automated pre-publication filter; a reported post is hidden until an administrator reviews it (§10). Content being available does not mean that we have verified or endorsed it.

(5) When we become aware of content that is unlawful or breaches the Community Guidelines, we act under the procedure in §8–§12. Our duties under the law and our liability for our own fault remain unaffected.

(6) If your content breaches the confirmations in (2) or third-party rights through your fault, and a third party therefore brings a justified claim against Sencé, you are liable only in proportion to your fault and within the limits in the Terms of Use §12. Your statutory rights as a consumer remain unaffected.

(7) We apply these rules carefully, impartially and proportionately, having regard to fundamental rights, including freedom of expression.

Additional rule by region

  • Türkiye: Under Law No. 5651, you are the content provider and Sencé acts as the hosting provider.

§4 Prohibited content and conduct

Do not share or do any of the following in the App. These rules apply to posts, comments, Moments, messages, profiles, Reviews and the AI Feature. We have no tolerance for objectionable content or harassment.

  1. Content that is unlawful under the law that applies to you or to us.
  2. Content praising or supporting terrorism or violent extremism.
  3. Child sexual abuse or content sexualising children.
  4. Intimate images shared without consent (including AI-generated fakes), and threats using them.
  5. Sexually explicit content.
  6. Inciting hatred or violence on grounds of race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability or origin.
  7. Harassment, bullying, threats and stalking.
  8. Graphic violence; encouraging self-harm or suicide.
  9. Fraud, phishing and impersonation.
  10. Spam; artificially inflating likes, follows or comments.
  11. Another person's personal data or location (e.g. home address, phone number) without their permission.
  12. Content infringing intellectual property rights.
  13. Reviews not based on genuine experience; commercial content that is misleading or does not disclose a benefit.
  14. Promotion of illegal goods and services; malware and harmful links.
  15. Using the AI Feature to generate content of these kinds or to bypass safety measures.

We recommend that you do not share your special categories of data (such as health, religion or political opinions); never share anyone else's. We take context into account for news, artistic and educational content.

Additional rules by region

  • Türkiye: Item 1 includes content constituting the offences in Article 8 of Law No. 5651.
  • United Kingdom: In the UK, against terrorism, child sexual abuse and other priority illegal content we apply our reporting procedure (§8), swift removal and Account measures (§14); the content moderation tools we use are described in §10 (4).

§5 Reviews

5.1 Scores and reviews on Hotel pages (from Nuitée)

(1) The scores and reviews on Hotel pages come from Nuitée; they do not belong to Sencé Users. We show the source next to each review. We do not write or edit reviews, or sort them into positive and negative.

(2) We publish only reviews whose source verifies the purchase or the stay. We do not show reviews where we cannot confirm that the source carries out such verification.

(3) The average score is out of 10 and is calculated by Nuitée. Category summaries are an analysis that Nuitée produces from reviews using artificial intelligence; they are labelled as such on screen.

(4) We do not pay or give any benefit to anyone in return for reviews. You can report a review you believe is fake (§8); we review it and remove it where necessary.

5.2 Reviews by Sencé Users

(1) A Review of a Hotel may be written only by the Booking Holder of a Booking made for that Hotel through the App whose stay dates have passed. We do not publish Reviews for which we cannot verify the purchase. What you write about a Hotel in a post, comment or Moment is not published as a Review on the Hotel page and does not count towards its rating; §4 (item 13) and §6 apply to such content.

(2) Write your Review based on your own experience. If you are the owner, an employee or a competitor of the Hotel, do not review it. Writing or commissioning Reviews in return for a benefit is prohibited.

(3) We publish Reviews whether they are positive or negative. We do not publish only those that breach the Community Guidelines (e.g. insults, personal data, advertising).

(4) Published Reviews stay online for at least one year and are ordered by an objective criterion such as date or rating. If it is verified that the Hotel resolved the problem, this is shown next to the Review.

(5) A Review is not removed merely because it is negative; claims that it is unlawful are reviewed under §8–§11.

(6) You can delete your own Review at any time.

(7) You can also reach the rules in this section through a link from the screen where Reviews are shown. (in this version Users cannot write Reviews in the App; the Reviews shown come from Nuitée)

Additional rules by region

  • EU/EEA:
  • 5.1: We do not ourselves check that reviews come from real guests; we rely on the source's verification.
  • 5.2: We ensure Reviews come from Booking Holders who actually purchased that Hotel through the App by matching them with the Booking record. The average rating is the simple average of published Reviews. (in this version Users cannot write Reviews in the App; the Reviews shown come from Nuitée)
  • United Kingdom:
  • 5.1: We do not ourselves check that reviews come from real guests; we rely on the source's verification.
  • 5.2: We take reasonable and proportionate steps to prevent and remove fake and misleading Reviews.

§6 Incentivised and sponsored content

(1) If you received money, a free or discounted stay, a gift, a prize draw or any other benefit for content, or if you have a commercial link with a business, state this at the start of the content in a way that is easy to see. Use the words "Ad" or "Promotion" and the name of the business.

(2) If you are the owner, an employee or a representative of a Hotel or place, disclose this link in your posts about it. Such persons may not write a Review of that Hotel (§5.2 (2)).

(3) Sencé does not give benefits in return for content or Reviews. If Sencé has its own advertising or sponsored content, it is labelled "Ad".

(4) We may label or remove undisclosed incentivised content or take action under §14.

Additional rule by region

  • Türkiye: If you post as a social media influencer, you comply with the labelling rules in Article 23/A of the Regulation on Commercial Advertising and Unfair Commercial Practices.

§7 Private messages

(1) Messages are not visible to other Users; only the parties to the conversation can see them. Sencé staff see messages only in the cases in (2). They are stored on our service provider's servers and encrypted in transit; they are not end-to-end encrypted.

(2) We do not read your messages on our own initiative or scan their content. Only if a message is reported do we review the reported message and, as far as needed to understand it, nearby messages in the same conversation. Where there is a legal obligation or an authority's order, we may review and disclose the relevant messages to the extent the law requires.

(3) A new message Notification shows the sender's name and the first 100 characters of the message. Notifications are delivered through Apple and Expo infrastructure. You can hide previews in your device's Notification settings.

(4) The Community Guidelines also apply to messages; do not send unsolicited commercial or bulk messages. Sencé never asks for your password, card or payment details by message.

(5) If you delete your Account, the messages you sent are deleted; messages sent to you remain in the sender's Account. Retention period for messages: until the sender's Account is deleted; statutory retention obligations remain unaffected.

§8 How to report

(1) Who and what? Anyone may report unlawful content; Users may also report content breaching the Community Guidelines. Posts, comments, Moments, messages, profiles, Reviews, AI responses and Users themselves can be reported. In this version the in-App "Report" option is available only on posts; you can report other content and Users to destek@sencetravel.com.

(2) How? Using the "Report" option next to the content, or by writing to destek@sencetravel.com. People without an Account use the e-mail route.

(3) What should a report include?

  • the exact location of the content (e.g. a link, a username or a screenshot);
  • your reasons for believing that the content is unlawful or breaches the Community Guidelines;
  • your name and e-mail address;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith that the information in the report is accurate and complete.

For child abuse reports, giving your name is optional.

(4) Receipt and outcome. If you report using the "Report" option in the App, we confirm receipt on screen straight away. In this version we do not tell you the outcome of the report (§11).

(5) Additional information for intellectual property and personality rights reports is in §9, and review times are in §10. If there is immediate danger, first call the local emergency number (§15). Frequently making manifestly unfounded reports is misuse (§14).

Additional rule by region

  • United States: An intimate image request should include the image's location, a statement of non-consent, your contact details and your signature (full name).

§9 Intellectual property and personality rights reports

9.1 Intellectual property

(1) If you believe content infringes your copyright, trade mark or other intellectual property right, you can submit a Content Report using the reporting option in the App or by writing to destek@sencetravel.com.

(2) Your report should include: the work or right you believe is infringed; the exact location of the content; that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them; your name and contact details; and a statement that the information is accurate and that you act in good faith.

(3) We review the report under §8 and §10. If we find the infringement sufficiently clear, we remove or disable access to the content; in this version we do not send a Notification to the person who shared it (§11).

(4) A person whose content was removed may Appeal if they believe they had the right to share it (§12). Disputes over rights are finally decided by the courts.

(5) §14 applies to Accounts that repeatedly infringe intellectual property rights. Knowingly false reports also breach the Community Guidelines.

9.2 Personality rights and private life

(1) If content contains an insult, a false and defamatory claim about you, or information or images exposing your private life, you can submit a Content Report. You do not need to be a Sencé User.

(2) In your report, state where the content is, which of your rights is infringed and how, and that the person in the content is you or someone you are authorised to act for.

(3) We review the report having regard to freedom of expression and whether the content serves news, criticism or the public interest. If it is clearly unlawful, we remove it or disable access to it.

(4) You can also request the removal of a photo or video in which you can be recognised as a personal data request (Privacy Policy §13).

(5) Your right to apply to the courts and competent authorities remains unaffected. We comply with their decisions (§15).

Additional rules by region

  • Türkiye:
  • 9.1: The application and public prosecutor routes under Additional Article 4 of the Law on Intellectual and Artistic Works remain available.
  • 9.2: For breaches of your privacy, you can apply directly to the competent Presidency under Article 9/A of Law No. 5651. On your application, access is blocked within four hours at the latest. You submit your request to a criminal judgeship of peace for a decision within 24 hours of the time you applied; the judge decides within 48 hours at the latest. Courts are available for other breaches of personality rights.
  • United States: 9.1: Our designated agent for copyright notices: not yet designated; you can send copyright notices to destek@sencetravel.com.

§10 Review and timing; no general monitoring obligation

(1) Timing. A person reviews the report carefully and impartially within 7 days. Reports about threats to life, child abuse and intimate images take priority.

(2) Intimate images: 48 hours at the latest. If an intimate image of you, or of a person you are authorised to act for, has been shared without consent (including AI-generated fakes) and you report it, we remove the image within 48 hours at the latest of a valid report. Within the same period, we also make reasonable efforts to find and remove known identical copies in the App. If we decide that the image is not an intimate image or that the person reporting is not authorised, in this version we do not tell you so; you can Appeal that decision (§11, §12).

(3) No person reviews User Content item by item before it is published, and we make no commitment to monitor all content. The rules of the law on this point for your region are in the additional rules by region below. When we become aware of unlawful content through a report, our own review or an authority's order, we act without delay.

(4) Tools. For content moderation we use Content Reports, Blocking and review by an administrator; in this version we do not use an automated pre-publication filter. A reported post is hidden from everyone (including its owner) until an administrator reviews it. Blocking works both ways; an Account blocked by an administrator cannot post, share Moments, comment or send messages. If we hide or remove your content, in this version we do not send you a Notification; you can Appeal (§11, §12).

(5) We may carry out reviews on our own initiative; this is not a commitment to monitor all content.

(6) We review private messages only in the cases in §7 (2).

Additional rules by region

  • Türkiye: As a hosting provider, we are not obliged to check the content we host or to investigate whether there is unlawful activity (Law No. 5651 Art. 5/1). When we learn that e-commerce content such as a hotel listing is unlawful, we remove it within 48 hours at the latest.
  • EU/EEA: Under Article 8 of the Digital Services Act (DSA), no general obligation to monitor or to actively seek facts indicating illegal activity can be imposed on us.
  • United Kingdom: For illegal content and intimate image reports we apply an expedited route and respond within 48 hours. In the United Kingdom, the 48-hour period in (2) is based on the rule added to the Online Safety Act by the Crime and Policing Act 2026. As required by the Online Safety Act, we take proportionate measures to minimise the time illegal content stays in the App; the content moderation tools we use are described in (4).
  • United States: In the United States, the 48-hour period in (2) is based on the TAKE IT DOWN Act.

§11 Decisions and statement of reasons

(1) When we hide or remove content, disable access to it, reduce its visibility, or restrict, suspend or close your Account, in this version we do not send you the Moderation Decision as a Notification. (in this version there are no tools to reduce visibility or restrict by country; content is hidden or removed for everyone)

(2) You can send questions about the decision and your Appeal to destek@sencetravel.com (§12).

(3) In this version we do not tell the person who made the report about the decision either; the route in (2) is also open to them. We reveal the reporter's identity to the person who shared the content only if the law requires it or if it is strictly necessary for you to understand the decision and to Appeal (e.g. the name of the rights holder in an intellectual property report). In intimate image, harassment and child safety reports, we never reveal the reporter's identity unless the law requires it.

(4) Where the law or an authority's order prohibits it (e.g. confidentiality of an investigation), we may withhold or delay this information.

Additional rules by region

  • EU/EEA: The out-of-court dispute settlement route for Moderation Decisions is in §12.
  • United Kingdom: Our complaints route under the Online Safety Act is in §12.

§12 Appeals and other redress

(1) Both the person who shared the content and the person who reported it may Appeal a Moderation Decision. An Appeal is free and can be made for at least six months after you learn of the decision (e.g. that the content was removed), by writing to destek@sencetravel.com; in this version there is no separate Appeal route within the App.

(2) A person reviews the Appeal; we do not decide it by automated means alone. We tell you the outcome with reasons within 14 days.

(3) If we uphold your Appeal, we reverse the decision without delay and restore the content or Account.

(4) An Appeal is optional; it does not limit your right to go to the courts, consumer bodies or competent authorities.

Additional rules by region

  • EU/EEA: For a dispute about a Moderation Decision, you may also go to an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under Article 21 of the Digital Services Act (DSA); we will engage in good faith.
  • United Kingdom: This Appeal route is our complaints procedure under the Online Safety Act; it is also used for illegal content, takedown and restriction decisions, our use of proactive technology and claims that we have not met our duties.
  • Türkiye:
  • The application route under Article 9/A of Law No. 5651 is available for breaches of privacy (§9.2), and the courts for other breaches of personality rights.
  • You may also report content you believe constitutes the offences listed in Article 8 of Law No. 5651 to the public prosecutor or the competent Presidency.
  • For content removed under a court or authority decision, the remedies against that decision provided by law remain available; for example, a decision given as a protective measure under Article 8 of Law No. 5651 may be challenged under the Code of Criminal Procedure. Such decisions are not Sencé's Moderation Decisions.

§13 Blocking and user controls

(1) You can block a User from their profile, post or comment, or from the message screen.

(2) A person you block cannot see your posts, Moments, comments or profile content; cannot send you messages, comment on your posts or follow you. Follow connections between you are removed. You will not see their content either. Your past messages with each other remain visible to both of you so that they can be reported if needed, but no new messages can be sent. This way, someone who blocks you cannot hide from you the messages they sent you.

(3) We do not tell the other person that you blocked them, but they may notice that they cannot access your content. We cannot fully prevent them from seeing your public content without signing in or through another Account.

(4) You can unblock someone under Settings → Privacy → Blocked users or from their profile. Once you unblock them, they can see your public content again and send you messages; follow connections do not come back automatically.

(5) Blocking is not reporting. If there are threats, harassment or unlawful content, also report it (§8).

§14 Repeated breaches and misuse

(1) We may restrict, suspend or close, after a prior warning, Accounts that repeatedly breach the rules or frequently share manifestly unlawful content. The number of warnings and the suspension periods are: a warning for the first breach; a sharing ban (administrator block) on repetition; closure of the Account for serious or continuing breaches; a breach is taken into account for 12 months. In this version restriction is applied through an administrator block: an Account blocked by an administrator cannot post, share Moments, comment or send messages, but can still view content.

(2) For serious breaches such as child sexual abuse, terrorist propaganda, non-consensual intimate images or serious threats of violence, we may act immediately without a warning.

(3) For people who frequently submit manifestly unfounded reports or Appeals, we may, after a prior warning, stop processing them for a reasonable period.

(4) In deciding, we consider the number, seriousness and timing of the breaches and the person's intent. §11 and §12 apply to every measure.

(5) Restricting or closing an Account does not cancel Bookings already made (Terms of Use §19). Your rights over your personal data continue (Privacy Policy §13).

(6) Account measures taken for reasons unrelated to content (e.g. suspected fraud or failure to meet the age requirement) are governed by the Terms of Use §19.

§15 Suspected crime and public authorities

(1) If we obtain information giving rise to a suspicion that a criminal offence threatening a person's life or safety has taken place, is taking place or is likely to take place, we report it without delay to the competent law enforcement or judicial authorities of the country concerned.

(2) We remove child sexual abuse content and report it to the competent authorities.

(3) We comply with orders of courts and competent authorities to remove content, disable access or provide information, following the procedures and time limits in the law. Unless the law or the authority prohibits it, we tell the affected User about the order, its reasons and redress options (§11).

(4) Authorities in Türkiye. We comply with requests for information, removal of content or blocking of access from competent authorities in Türkiye, such as courts, prosecutors and police, the Personal Data Protection Board, the Ministry of Trade, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism and the Cyber Security Directorate, after examining their legal basis and the authority's competence.

(5) Foreign authorities. We examine, as to the authority's competence and the legal basis, orders to act against illegal content and orders to provide information issued under the Digital Services Act by judicial and administrative authorities of EU Member States, requests from Ofcom under the UK Online Safety Act, and requests from authorities of other countries. We comply without undue delay with requests we find lawful and inform the authority of how we have complied. For a request that is clearly unlawful, outside the authority's competence or excessive, we ask for clarification, object or refuse it. We disclose your personal data to a foreign authority only to the extent the applicable law permits.

(6) We share only the data necessary for these purposes. We record each request, our assessment, our response and the dates.

(7) If you or someone else is in immediate danger, first call the local emergency number.

(8) The point of contact for authorities is in §16.

§17 Child safety and age

(1) To open an Account you must be at least 18 years old. You declare your age when opening an Account.

(2) To make a Booking you must be 18 or older; persons under 18 cannot make a Booking (Terms of Use §7).

(3) If we learn that someone under the minimum age has opened an Account, we will close it and delete their data in accordance with the Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy. Legal representatives can report this to destek@sencetravel.com.

(4) The applicable law governs this matter.

(5) Child sexual abuse and content sexualising children are prohibited (§4). You can report such content without giving your name (§8); the report is reviewed with priority (§10). We remove the content and report it to the competent authorities (§15); we may take measures against the Account without a warning (§14). You can also send complaints and questions about child safety to destek@sencetravel.com.

§18 Changes

(1) We change this document only for a valid reason: a change in law, regulation or a court or authority decision; adding, changing or removing a feature; security and fighting abuse; a change in the terms of our providers such as Nuitée; making the text clearer or correcting errors.

(2) We will notify you of a change in the App and by e-mail at least 30 days before it takes effect. The notice will summarise what has changed.

(3) For any change that narrows your rights or increases your obligations, we will ask for your express consent. Continuing to use the App does not by itself count as consent. If you do not consent, you may end the contract by deleting your Account free of charge (Terms of Use §19).

(4) Changes required by law or by an authority's decision, or that address an urgent security risk, may take effect sooner; we will inform you as soon as possible. If such a change narrows your rights, your right to exit free of charge under (3) is preserved.

(5) We state each version's number and effective date at the top of the document. We publish this document and its previous versions at https://legal.sencetravel.com in a form that is public, easily accessible and machine-readable. We record which version you accepted and when.
