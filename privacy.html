§1 Summary

  • This Policy explains how we process your personal data in the App and on the https://legal.sencetravel.com site. It is the umbrella document for your personal data; the KVKK Information Notice, the Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy, the Cookie and SDK Notice and the Explicit Consent Texts form part of it. If they differ, the one more favourable to you applies.
  • This text is for your information; we do not ask for your approval, we only record that you have seen it.
  • We do not see your card details. Nuitée, the Seller, takes payment for Hotel Bookings; Booking details go to Nuitée and the Hotel.
  • Your data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt) and accessed by Sencé in Türkiye. Some of our providers are in the US; your Booking details go to Nuitée and to the country where the Hotel is located (see §10).
  • What you write to the AI Feature goes to Google. We do not add your name, email or IP address to it; anything you type into the text yourself does go (see §8).
  • We do not sell your data or show targeted advertising. Analytics runs only with your permission.
  • You can delete your Account in the App; deletion is completed within 30 days at the latest. Records the law requires us to keep are kept.
  • Questions and requests: destek@sencetravel.com.

§7 Public information, location, User Content and Notification content

§7.1 Public information and post location

  • Public: your profile (name, username, profile photo, bio), posts, Moments (24 hours on the global map; on your profile map until you delete or archive them), comments, likes, route and trip lists. In this version there is no separate visibility setting for your profile or content.
  • Only people in the conversation: direct messages. Only you: the history of regions you have visited, your saved items, your price alerts.
  • Post location: You must choose a place to share a post, and the place you choose is published with the post; the "Share on map" setting does not change this. If the post matches a known place, that place's location is shown; otherwise a point shifted by about 250 metres; for Moments the shift is about 500 metres. A shifted location still shows your area; keep this in mind when posting near your home.
  • Photo file: We remove information your device writes into photos, such as where and when they were taken and the device model (EXIF), before upload.
  • Public content can be copied by others; deleting it from Sencé does not remove those copies.

§7.2 Location permission

  • Location permission is an operating system permission that gives the App access to your device's location; it is not Explicit Consent under KVKK. We process your location to provide the features you ask for (see §5).
  • We use location only while the App is open; we do not collect location in the background.
  • Your location is used to show nearby places and Hotels, open the map and, if you wish, add a location to your post; for this it is sent to Google and Mapbox.
  • If you do not allow it, you can continue by choosing a city or place manually. Sharing a Moment, however, requires location permission. You can turn the permission off in your device settings or share only your approximate location.

§7.3 Camera, photo and microphone permission

  • These are operating system permissions; they are not Explicit Consent. They are used only for an action you start: taking a profile photo or a post, choosing photos or videos from your gallery, adding sound to a video post.
  • When you choose a post, we show the photos and videos in your gallery on the selection screen; we upload only the files you select.
  • Location data embedded in photos is removed before upload (see §7.1).
  • If you choose to add your Booking to your device calendar, calendar access is used only on your device; your calendar data does not reach us. We do not access your contacts and we do not receive biometric data such as Face ID.
  • If you do not allow it, only the related action does not work; you can turn the permissions off in your device settings.

§7.4 What Notifications contain

If you turn on Notifications, a Notification may contain:

  • for a new message, the sender's name and the first 100 characters of the message;
  • for a comment, the commenter's name and the first 80 characters of the comment;
  • for likes, follows and tags, the name of the person concerned;
  • for a Booking update, a Review request or a price alert, the Hotel's name.

Notifications reach your device through Expo and Apple's notification service (see §9) and may appear on your lock screen. You can hide previews in your device settings or turn Notifications off. In this version there is no in-App option to hide message previews.

§10 Transfers abroad

Sencé is operated from Türkiye. Your data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt) and some of our service providers are in the US (see §9). Your data is therefore transferred abroad.

For a Hotel Booking, your Booking details go to Nuitée and from there to the country where the Hotel is located. That country may be outside Türkiye and the EU. Hotels receive your data from Nuitée, not from us; we do not sign a separate contract with each Hotel.

Users in Türkiye (KVKK Article 9)

  • At the date of this text, the Personal Data Protection Board has not announced an adequacy decision for the countries we transfer data to; we therefore do not rely on an adequacy decision.
  • Each transfer relies on the legal ground we set out for that purpose in the table in §5.
  • As the safeguard, we will use the standard contracts announced by the Board: a "controller to processor" standard contract with providers that process data on our behalf, and a "controller to controller" standard contract with recipients that process your data for their own purposes.
  • As of the publication date of this version, these contracts have not yet been signed or notified to the Personal Data Protection Authority.
  • Onward transfers by our recipients to other countries are subject to the same safeguards. Hotels receive your data from Nuitée, not from us; the transfer to the Hotel is subject to the onward transfer rules in our standard contract with Nuitée. We do not sign a separate contract with each Hotel.
  • These transfers are part of the ordinary operation of the Services and happen regularly. They are therefore not incidental (occasional) transfers and do not rely on your Explicit Consent. We do not ask for your consent for them.
  • If an incidental transfer is needed, we make it only for that transfer and only on one of the grounds in Article 9/6 of the KVKK. If that ground is your Explicit Consent, we explain the possible risks of the transfer in advance and ask for your approval with a separate statement. Example: forwarding a request of yours, at your request, to a body abroad with which we have no regular relationship.
  • You enter your card details directly into Nuitée's payment form; they are not transferred through us.

Users in the EU/EEA

  • The data you enter in the App is stored by our EU host and accessed by Sencé in Türkiye. The European Commission has not adopted an adequacy decision for Türkiye. For the flow from our EU host to Sencé: no transfer tool has yet been signed as of the publication date of this version.
  • For transfers to providers in the US, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework if the provider is on its list, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. As of the publication date of this version, the tool used for each provider has not yet been documented.

Users in the United Kingdom

  • For transfers to providers in the US, we rely on the UK Extension to the Data Privacy Framework if the provider participates in it; otherwise on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.
  • Transfers to our EU host rely on the adequacy the United Kingdom recognises for the EU/EEA. For the flow to Sencé in Türkiye: no transfer tool has yet been signed as of the publication date of this version.

Copy of the safeguards. You can ask for a copy of the safeguards we use by writing to destek@sencetravel.com. We may remove parts that contain trade secrets.

§11 Retention

We keep your data for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it is processed, or for the period required by law. When the period ends, we erase or destroy the data or make it anonymous.

  • Pseudonymised data is not anonymous. Data that does not carry your name but can be linked to you with additional information remains personal data. Booking records kept after your Account is deleted, with the link to your Account removed and contact details erased, are of this kind; we protect them as personal data and destroy them when their period ends.
  • Records kept for a statutory period are kept only for that statutory purpose and with restricted access.
  • Periodic destruction. We destroy data whose retention period has ended at regular intervals; the interval is no later than 3 months from when the obligation to destroy arises. We record every destruction and keep these records for at least 3 years.

§12 Account deletion

You can delete your Account at any time, free of charge and without giving reasons: in the App via Settings → Delete Account; on the web at https://legal.sencetravel.com/delete-account.html; or by writing to destek@sencetravel.com. Details are in the Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy.

§12.1 After you ask to delete your Account

  • When you confirm your request, you are signed out and your Account is placed on hold for 30 days. During this time your profile and content are not visible to other Users. In this version your profile and content remain visible during the waiting period.
  • In this version there is no in-App way to withdraw the request; after the request you cannot sign in for 30 days, and at the end of that period your Account is deleted.
  • When the waiting period ends, deletion starts and is completed no later than 30 days after your request. If the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies to you, this period is 30 days or one month, whichever ends first. (deletion is completed by a daily job that runs after the waiting period ends, so it may run into day 31)
  • In this version no e-mail is sent when we receive a deletion request or when deletion is complete; the records we continue to keep and why are set out in §12.2.
  • Deleting your Account does not cancel your Bookings; if you want to cancel, do so first. After deletion you can write to destek@sencetravel.com about a Booking.
  • Records we are required by law to keep are not deleted; we list them and the reasons below (see §12.2).

§12.2 What we delete and what we keep

  • When deletion is complete, your Account, your User Content (including photos and videos), messages you sent, your follow links, your Blocking, your price alerts, the history of regions you visited and, if you gave permission, your analytics data are deleted from our systems. The full list of what is deleted is in the Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy.
  • Booking and transaction records, pre-contract information and confirmation records, consent and opt-out records, invoices and accounting documents, request, complaint and support records, Content Report and moderation records (Content Reports you made and those made about your content; decision and reasons) (in this version Content Reports you made are deleted together with your Account), traffic data and the destruction record are not deleted. We keep them only for the statutory purpose, with restricted access and for the periods in §11. In Booking records, the link to your Account is removed and contact, Guest and special request details are erased. The remaining records are pseudonymised, not anonymous. The full list and the legal bases are in the Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy.

§12.3 Notice to providers and backups

  • In this version no automatic deletion request is sent to the providers that process data on our behalf (error monitoring Sentry, notifications Expo, e-mail Resend); analytics (PostHog) is switched off in this version. If you signed in with Apple, your Apple sign-in authorisation is revoked during deletion.
  • No information identifying your Account is sent to the AI Feature and map providers (Google, Mapbox); data passed to them is deleted at the end of their contractual periods.
  • Nuitée and the Hotel keep Booking data for their own obligations under their own responsibility. We pass your deletion request to Nuitée; you can also contact Nuitée directly.
  • If you signed in with Apple or Google, we remove the link with Sencé; those accounts are not deleted.
  • Copies in backups are kept only to protect against data loss, are not used for any other purpose and are deleted automatically within 7 days. If a backup is restored, deletion is applied again. (we do this manually using the deletion audit records)
  • If you ask, we tell you which recipients we passed your request to.

§17 Security and breaches

§17.1 How we protect your data

We protect your data against unauthorised access, loss and misuse with technical and organisational measures. Some of them:

  • your card details never reach us; they are entered directly into Nuitée's payment form;
  • the connection between the App and our servers is encrypted;
  • database access rules ensure that each User can access only the data open to them; public information (see §7.1) is an exception;
  • email and IP address do not enter error logs; error and analytics logs use your Account ID (a unique identifier linked to your Account) instead of your name (these logs are not anonymous);
  • your identity, email and IP address are not sent to the AI Feature;
  • only people who need data for their work can access it;
  • we have written contracts with providers that process data on our behalf.

No system is completely secure. Use a strong password, do not share it, and tell us about anything suspicious (see §17.3).

§17.2 If there is a data breach

If we learn that your personal data has been obtained by unauthorised persons:

  • we investigate and take immediate steps to limit the harm;
  • we notify the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board without delay and no later than 72 hours after we become aware;
  • once we have identified the people affected, we let you know as soon as reasonably possible: directly if we have your contact details, otherwise through channels such as our website;
  • we tell you what happened, which data was affected, the likely consequences, the measures we took, what you can do and how to reach us;
  • we handle a breach at a provider that processes data on our behalf in the same way; our providers must notify us without delay.

Additional rules for users in the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and the United States are in §20.

§17.3 Reporting a security incident

If you suspect that someone else has accessed your Account, that your data has been exposed or that there is a security vulnerability in the Services, email destek@sencetravel.com with the subject "Security".

  • Tell us what happened, when you noticed it and which Account is involved. Do not send your password or card details; we will never ask for them.
  • If someone else has accessed your Account, change your password immediately; if you cannot sign in, write to us and we will secure your Account. In this version locking and signing out sessions are done manually on your request to support.
  • We acknowledge your message within 24 hours and tell you the outcome of our review. If the incident is a data breach, we follow the steps in §17.2.
  • We ask anyone reporting a vulnerability in good faith to give us a chance to fix it and not to access other people's data.
  • Writing to us does not affect your right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.
